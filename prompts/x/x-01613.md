## Prompt

```text
Create a 30-second cinematic autumn lifestyle video featuring a young woman in a peaceful forest beside a flowing stream, surrounded by golden-orange autumn leaves. She wears a cozy dark knitted sweater, a cream-colored scarf, and a light skirt. The atmosphere is warm, dreamy, peaceful, and natural, with soft golden sunlight filtering through the trees.

Scene 1 (0–5 seconds): Wide cinematic establishing shot of the woman standing beside a small flowing river in a golden autumn forest. She gently looks upward toward the falling leaves, enjoying the peaceful scenery. Slow cinematic camera movement, natural wind moving her hair and the leaves.

Scene 2 (5–8 seconds): Transition into a close-up of the woman holding a beautiful golden maple leaf with both hands. She looks at it with a gentle smile, admiring its details. Soft background bokeh, warm sunlight illuminating the leaf.

Scene 3 (8–13 seconds): Medium-wide shot of the woman standing beside the river, holding the leaf while golden leaves drift slowly through the air. The river flows gently in the background. Smooth cinematic camera movement.

Scene 4 (13–17 seconds): She slowly crouches beside the river and gently places the maple leaf onto the water. Capture the delicate interaction, subtle ripples, reflections of golden trees, and the leaf floating away naturally.

Scene 5 (17–21 seconds): Cinematic rear and side shot of the woman standing beside the river, watching the water and autumn scenery. Her long hair moves gently in the breeze as the sunlight creates a magical golden glow.

Scene 6 (21–25 seconds): She slowly turns toward the camera with a warm, natural smile. Medium close-up, realistic facial expressions, soft sunlight, beautifully blurred golden forest background.

Scene 7 (25–30 seconds): Final intimate close-up portrait. She gently smiles with relaxed eyes while golden autumn leaves and warm sunlight create a dreamy cinematic bokeh behind her. Slow camera push-in, subtle natural movement, peaceful ending.

Visual style: Ultra-realistic cinematic photography, warm autumn color grading, golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, natural skin texture, realistic hair movement, smooth camera motion, peaceful storytelling, photorealistic environment, high cinematic quality, 4K detail, seamless scene transitions, no text, no watermark.
```
