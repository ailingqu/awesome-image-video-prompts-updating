## Prompt

```text
Create an ultra-realistic 30-second live-action Korean revenge-comedy scene inside a luxurious Korean restaurant.

CHARACTERS:
Woman: Stylish Korean woman in her 20s wearing a red blazer, black mini skirt, and long black hair.
Man: Arrogant Korean businessman wearing an expensive navy suit and gold watch.

ACTION SEQUENCE — EXACTLY 30 SECONDS:

0–5 seconds: The woman sits peacefully at a restaurant table, enjoying a large bowl of steaming noodles.

5–9 seconds: The arrogant man walks past and deliberately knocks her bowl onto the table, spilling noodles everywhere. He laughs mockingly.

9–13 seconds: She slowly looks at the mess, then looks directly at him with a serious expression.

13–18 seconds: She picks up a small bowl of cold noodles and places it carefully on a serving tray. As the man turns around, she accidentally-on-purpose bumps the tray, sending noodles flying onto his expensive suit.

18–23 seconds: The man freezes in disbelief. Restaurant customers turn around. The woman calmly hands him a napkin and says: "Now we're even."

23–30 seconds: He tries to walk away proudly, but steps on a fallen noodle and stumbles into an empty chair. The woman continues eating as if nothing happened. End with her satisfied smile.

SOUND DESIGN: Restaurant ambience, chopsticks, ceramic bowl impact, noodles splashing, crowd reactions, footsteps, chair movement, natural dialogue.

VISUAL REQUIREMENTS: Photorealistic live-action, realistic food physics, detailed skin textures, natural facial expressions, consistent characters and clothing. Exactly 30 seconds. No animation, no supernatural effects.
```
