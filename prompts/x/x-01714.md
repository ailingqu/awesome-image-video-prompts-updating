## Prompt

```text
Create an ultra-realistic winter portrait photograph of a beautiful young adult woman sitting in a snow-covered outdoor setting surrounded by dense evergreen pine trees heavily blanketed in fresh snow. She has natural, shoulder-length dark brown hair with snowflakes resting gently on it, delicate facial features, rosy cheeks, and a warm, genuine smile with slightly squinted eyes.

She wears a soft, oversized light blue teddy-fleece winter coat, a thick fluffy pastel pink knitted scarf wrapped comfortably around her neck, and white fluffy gloves. She gently holds her hands close to her body, creating a cozy winter pose.

Capture the scene as an intimate close-up portrait from a slightly elevated camera angle, with her face as the main focal point. Soft snowflakes fall naturally throughout the frame, with some appearing slightly blurred in the foreground for depth. The background features beautiful snow-covered pine branches.

Use soft, diffused natural winter daylight, cool pastel tones, realistic skin texture, subtle pink undertones, delicate highlights, and a dreamy snowy atmosphere. Authentic candid photography, shallow depth of field, professional DSLR quality, beautifully balanced composition, photorealistic details, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, no text, no watermark.
```
