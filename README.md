## How to use a prompt

1. Open a card and copy the block under **Prompt**.
2. Go to **[vinano.ai](https://vinano.ai?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=awesome-image-video-prompts-updating&utm_content=how-to)**, paste it into the image or video generator and pick a model.
3. Replace placeholders such as brand, product, city or person with your own details, then generate.
4. Need it in code? Every prompt is also in [`prompts/prompts.json`](prompts/prompts.json); system prompts for building your own generator: [EN](prompts/system-prompt-en.md) · [中文](prompts/system-prompt-zh.md).
